# 7. Customer discovery

**Goal:** find out if owners have this problem badly enough to pay, *before* building much. Talk to 5 owners or managers in Phase 0.

## Rules for interviews

- Ask about **past behavior**, not opinions about the future. "When did that last happen?" beats "Would you use…?"
- Don't pitch until the end. Learn first.
- Write notes straight after, in `docs/interviews/YYYY-MM-DD-<venue>.md`.

## Script (about 20 minutes)

**Context**
1. How many tables? Busiest days and hours?
2. How do you decide how many staff to put on each shift? When did you last get it wrong?

**Seating and service**
3. What happens when guests sit down? How does a waiter know?
4. Have guests ever left because nobody came? How often? How do you know?
5. How long do tables usually sit uncleared on a busy night?

**Waiting list**
6. When there's a queue, how does the host estimate wait times? How accurate is it?

**Tools and money**
7. What POS / booking / waitlist tools do you use? What do they *not* tell you?
8. What have you paid for in the last year to improve operations?

**Cameras**
9. What cameras do you have? Who installed them? Can you view them on your phone? (Gets at brand, NVR, RTSP access.)
10. How would staff feel about a system that reads table status from the cameras?

**Only at the end, a short pitch**
11. "If you got a weekly report showing when you're really busy, how long guests wait before being served, and how long tables sit dirty, would that change anything you do?"
12. "What would that be worth per month?" (Get a number.)

## Pilot offer

> Free 2–4 week pilot. We analyze your footage on-site and give you a staffing and table-turnover report. In return: permission to record a few days of footage for R&D, under a written data agreement, deleted by an agreed date.

## What we need to learn (scorecard)

| Question | Evidence that counts |
|----------|---------------------|
| Is "unserved guests" a real, frequent pain? | Specific recent stories, not "yeah, probably" |
| Is "dirty table time" a real pain? | Same |
| Would they pay for the report alone? | A number in €/month |
| Are cameras usable? | Model + RTSP access confirmed |
| Will staff accept it? | Owner's honest guess plus any staff reaction |
| What do they use today? | Tool names, and what's missing |
