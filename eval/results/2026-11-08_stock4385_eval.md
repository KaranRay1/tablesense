## Notes (written by hand)

**Not a product accuracy number.** 15 s time-lapse, 4 tables, labeled by eye at 0.25-0.5 s resolution, timers scaled down (`configs/stock_4385.yaml`). It shows the evaluation works on real pipeline output, and what fails.

- **T2, 53%: confirms the known hard case** "waiter clears within the AWAY hold" (docs/04-state-machine.md). Guests left at 6.3 s, the table was cleared by 7.0 s, inside the 1.5 s hold. The system showed yellow from 7.9 s and only went green at the 12.9 s timeout (`assumed_clear`). 5 s of yellow on a clean table.
- **T3, 3 missed changes:** yellow and green phases under 0.5 s, shorter than the hold, so they're undetectable by design. In real time these would be minutes.
- **T4, 89.6%:** the group was detected 1.4 s after sitting (t_seat = 1 s, plus smoothing).
- **No wrongly-yellow time:** the T3 detection dropout became a hidden AWAY, not yellow.

**What this means for the product:** fast clearing during the hold is now the most important state-machine problem. Measure how often it happens on real footage, then decide between staff detection and table-top (plates) detection.
